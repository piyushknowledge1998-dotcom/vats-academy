Why topic-wise?

Don't just take a test. Find the gap.

A full-length test tells you how you performed. A topic-wise book can help answer a more useful question: where exactly is the weakness?

By placing questions inside a topic structure, the product is intended to support targeted practice, revision and repeated exposure to the kinds of demands that appear in the examination.

01

Locate

Identify the exact topic or sub-topic where performance needs attention.

02

Practise

Work through focused question sets instead of repeatedly taking only broad tests.

03

Review

Use answer keys and explanations where they are included in the final product.

04

Revise

Return to the underlying concept and reattempt the area after revision.

Designed for

  • Topic-level revision before an examination.
  • Targeted practice after completing a chapter or unit.
  • PYQ-driven understanding of what an examination actually asks.
  • Repeated practice across the preparation cycle.

Product architecture

Four layers of practice.

The structure can be adapted for different examinations and subjects while keeping the same underlying workflow.

01ScopeExamination, paper, subject, topic and sub-topic are defined before practice begins.
02QuestionsPrevious-year questions are grouped for focused practice, with additional practice clearly labelled when used.
03ReviewAnswer keys and explanations can turn an attempt into a learning opportunity.
04RevisionWeak areas feed back into notes, concept revision and another practice cycle.

The larger ecosystem

Books today. A test engine tomorrow.
